Paper Digest · arXiv 2011.14672 · CVPR 2021

HybrIK: A Hybrid Analytical-Neural Inverse Kinematics Solution for 3D Human Pose and Shape Estimation

Jiefeng Li, Chao Xu, Zhicun Chen, Siyuan Bian, Lixin Yang, Cewu Lu
Skeleton-to-SMPLX SMPL IK Twist-and-Swing 3D Pose + Mesh CVPR 2021
arXiv PDF

Core Idea

  • Hybrid IK bridge: combines strengths of 3D keypoint localization and parametric mesh realism by mapping joints to SMPL rotations through IK decomposition.
    混合 IK 桥接：通过 IK 分解把关节点映射到 SMPL 旋转，连接 3D 关键点定位精度与参数化人体网格结构合理性。
  • Swing analytically solved: relative swing rotations are solved from predicted 3D joints.
    Swing 解析求解：相对 swing 旋转由预测的 3D 关节位置直接解析求得。
  • Twist learned from image cues: twist components are predicted by neural network features.
    Twist 由视觉学习：twist 分量通过神经网络从图像特征中估计。

Method Sketch

Image → 3D joints → swing (analytic) + twist (neural) → SMPL pose parameters → mesh and 3D pose

Why It Matters in This Bank

  • Foundational IK line: HybrIK is the core predecessor for later whole-body extensions such as HybrIK-X.
    IK 基础路线：HybrIK 是后续 HybrIK-X 等全身扩展方法的基础前身。
  • Practical impact: paper reports strong gains over prior methods on 3D pose/mesh benchmarks while preserving model plausibility.
    实践影响：论文报告在 3D 姿态/网格基准上较前作有明显提升，同时保持人体模型合理性。

TL;DR

HybrIK (CVPR 2021) is a key IK paper that fuses analytical and neural components: swing from joint geometry, twist from visual learning. In your taxonomy, it is the canonical early entry for the Skeleton-to-SMPLX line and a direct precursor to HybrIK-X.
