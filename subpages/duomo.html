DuoMo: Dual Motion Diffusion for World-Space Human Reconstruction

Yufu Wang, Evonne Ng, Soyong Shin, Rawal Khirodkar, Yuan Dong, Zhaoen Su, Jinhyung Park, Kris Kitani, Alexander Richard, Fabian Prada, Michael Zollhofer

Core Idea

  • DuoMo factorizes world-space human reconstruction into two diffusion stages to balance robustness and global consistency.
    DuoMo 将世界坐标人体重建分解为两阶段 diffusion，以同时兼顾鲁棒性与全局一致性。
  • Stage 1 estimates motion in camera coordinates from noisy/incomplete video observations.
    第一阶段在相机坐标系中从噪声/缺失观测中估计人体运动。
  • Stage 2 lifts and refines this proposal in world coordinates for globally coherent trajectories.
    第二阶段将结果提升到世界坐标并进行全局一致性修正。

Method Snapshot

Unconstrained monocular video → camera-space diffusion model → lifted world-space proposal → world-space diffusion refinement → globally consistent human motion
The paper emphasizes direct mesh-vertex motion generation rather than relying only on parametric body models.

TL;DR

DuoMo is a two-stage diffusion framework for world-space human reconstruction from monocular video. It first predicts camera-space motion, then refines it in world coordinates, improving robustness under noisy or incomplete observations while keeping global motion consistency.
