Key Contributions

  • StepSplat — incremental Gaussian update (0.26s): Instead of rebuilding the full Gaussian scene from scratch at each step, StepSplat dynamically updates only the newly revealed Gaussian splats, reusing prior geometry and drastically reducing per-step cost.
    StepSplat — 增量 Gaussian 更新（0.26s）：不在每步重建完整 Gaussian 场景，StepSplat 仅动态更新新暴露的 Gaussian splats，复用已有几何，大幅降低逐步开销。
  • QuickDepth — lightweight depth completion module: A fast, lightweight network that provides geometrically consistent depth estimates for newly inpainted regions, replacing the expensive guided depth diffusion in WonderWorld without sacrificing spatial accuracy.
    QuickDepth — 轻量深度补全模块：快速轻量网络为新 inpaint 区域提供几何一致的深度估计，替换 WonderWorld 中昂贵的引导式深度扩散，同时不牺牲空间精度。
  • FastPaint — 2-step diffusion inpainting: A distilled inpainting diffusion model that runs in only 2 denoising steps instead of the standard 20–50, tuned specifically to maintain spatial appearance consistency for scene disocclusion regions.
    FastPaint — 两步扩散 inpainting：蒸馏后的 inpainting 扩散模型仅需 2 步去噪（而非标准 20-50 步），专门调优以维持场景去遮挡区域的空间外观一致性。
  • First real-time interactive 3D scene generation: The combination of StepSplat + QuickDepth + FastPaint achieves 0.72s end-to-end, enabling truly real-time user-driven scene exploration — a first in the field.
    首个实时交互 3D 场景生成：StepSplat + QuickDepth + FastPaint 组合实现 0.72s 端到端延迟，首次在该领域实现真正的实时用户驱动场景探索。

Method / Architecture

Current 3DGS scene + camera move → Render: detect disocclusion holes → QuickDepth: fast depth completion (hole → depth) → FastPaint: 2-step diffusion inpainting (hole → RGB) → StepSplat: incremental Gaussian update (0.26s) → Updated 3DGS scene (total: 0.72s)

WonderWorld bottleneck analysis: WonderWorld's ~10s latency breaks down into: (a) guided depth diffusion for hole filling (~7s), (b) full Gaussian scene re-optimization (~2s), (c) RGB inpainting (~1s). WonderTurbo replaces all three with faster alternatives.

StepSplat (0.26s): Incrementally adds new Gaussian primitives only for newly exposed pixels, anchored to the existing scene graph. No re-optimization of old Gaussians is needed; the update is a feed-forward pass over the new region.

QuickDepth: A lightweight encoder-decoder conditioned on rendered partial depth that completes the hole region in a single forward pass. Replaces the multi-step guided depth diffusion of WonderWorld.

FastPaint (2-step diffusion): Uses progressive distillation / consistency distillation to reduce the inpainting diffusion from standard 20+ steps to 2 steps. The model is fine-tuned to prioritize spatial boundary consistency over global photorealism, which is critical for seamless scene compositing.

WonderWorld 瓶颈分析：WonderWorld 约 10s 延迟分解为：(a) 引导式深度扩散补洞约 7s，(b) 完整 Gaussian 场景重优化约 2s，(c) RGB inpainting 约 1s。WonderTurbo 以更快方案逐一替换。

StepSplat（0.26s）：仅为新暴露像素增量添加 Gaussian 基元，锚定到已有场景图，无需重新优化旧 Gaussians，更新是对新区域的前向传播。

QuickDepth：以已渲染部分深度为条件的轻量编解码器，单次前向传播补全空洞区域深度，替换 WonderWorld 的多步引导深度扩散。

FastPaint（两步扩散）：使用渐进蒸馏/一致性蒸馏将 inpainting 扩散从标准 20+ 步压缩到 2 步，微调时优先保证空间边界一致性而非全局真实感，对无缝场景合成至关重要。

Limitations

  • Quality vs speed trade-off: The 2-step FastPaint and single-pass QuickDepth sacrifice some visual quality compared to WonderWorld's full diffusion pipeline — fine texture details and depth boundary accuracy are reduced.
    质量与速度权衡：两步 FastPaint 和单次 QuickDepth 与 WonderWorld 完整扩散管线相比牺牲了部分视觉质量 — 精细纹理细节和深度边界精度有所下降。
  • Incremental geometry drift: StepSplat's incremental updates can accumulate geometric errors over many steps, especially in scenes with large camera translations or complex occlusion patterns.
    增量几何漂移：StepSplat 的增量更新在多步后可能积累几何误差，尤其是在摄像机大幅平移或复杂遮挡模式的场景中。
  • Not dynamic: Like WonderWorld, WonderTurbo generates static scenes only — dynamic objects, physics, or temporal effects require WonderPlay's hybrid simulator approach.
    仅限静态：与 WonderWorld 一样，WonderTurbo 只生成静态场景 — 动态物体、物理或时序效果需要 WonderPlay 的混合仿真器方案。

TL;DR

WonderTurbo (ICCV 2025, arXiv 2504.02261) achieves 0.72s interactive 3D scene generation — a 15× speedup over WonderWorld — through three targeted optimizations: StepSplat (incremental Gaussian update in 0.26s), QuickDepth (lightweight depth completion replacing guided diffusion), and FastPaint (2-step distilled inpainting diffusion). It is the first real-time interactive 3D scene generation system. Its engineering breakdown is valuable reference material for understanding where WonderWorld's computational budget is spent and how to reduce it.
