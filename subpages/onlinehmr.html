Yiwen Zhao, Ce Zheng, Yufu Wang, Hsueh-Han Daniel Yang, Liting Wen, Laszlo A. Jeni

Core Problem

  • Most world-coordinate video HMR methods are offline and depend on future frames or global optimization.
    大多数世界坐标视频 HMR 仍是离线方案，依赖未来帧或全局优化。
  • This limits interactive use cases like AR/VR and telepresence where low latency and strict causality are required.
    这限制了 AR/VR、远程临场等对低延迟与因果性要求严格的交互场景。

Method Summary

Streaming monocular video → camera-coordinate online HMR branch + human-centric incremental SLAM branch → online world-grounded mesh + camera trajectory
Key designs: causal key-value cache, sliding-window learning, physically plausible trajectory correction.

TL;DR

OnlineHMR turns world-grounded video HMR from offline to real online processing. It keeps causal, streaming inference through cache-based temporal modeling and incremental SLAM, while reaching accuracy close to chunk-based offline approaches.
