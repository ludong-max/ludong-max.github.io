Egocentric Whole-Body Human Mesh Recovery with Prior-Guided Learning

Soyeon Na, Seung Young Noh, Ju Yong Chang
Ego-to-Exo SMPLX Regression Egocentric Estimation Whole-Body SMPL-X Prior-Guided Learning ICIP 2026

What It Solves

  • Targets monocular egocentric whole-body mesh recovery where reliable SMPL/SMPL-X GT is scarce.
    面向单目自我中心全身网格恢复，重点解决 SMPL/SMPL-X 真实标注稀缺问题。
  • Goes beyond body-only pseudo-GT pipelines by improving hand/face detail recovery in full-body mesh estimation.
    相比仅关注身体姿态的伪标注方案，强化手部与面部细节恢复。

Method Snapshot

Egocentric image → deterministic fisheye undistortion → exocentric whole-body foundation adaptation + diffusion pose prior → SMPL-X whole-body mesh
Training leverages optimization-based pseudo-GT aligned with 3D joint supervision.

TL;DR

This work is a strong egocentric whole-body SMPL-X entry: it combines better optimization-based pseudo-GT, exocentric foundation priors, and diffusion pose priors, with explicit fisheye handling for head-mounted cameras.
