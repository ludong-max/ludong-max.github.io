DPoser-X: Diffusion Model as Robust 3D Whole-body Human Pose Prior

Junzhe Lu, Jing Lin, Hongkun Dou, Ailing Zeng, Yue Deng, Xian Liu, Zhongang Cai, Lei Yang, Yulun Zhang, Haoqian Wang, Ziwei Liu
Single-View SMPLX regression Optimization / Prior Diffusion Prior Whole-body Pose arXiv 2025
arXiv PDF
One-sentence problem: How can we build a robust and versatile whole-body pose prior (body + hand + face) when high-quality full-body datasets are scarce and highly articulated pose space is hard to model?
一句话问题：在高质量全身数据稀缺、姿态空间高度复杂的情况下，如何构建兼顾身体、手部和面部的稳健全身姿态先验？

Key Contributions

  • DPoser-X prior: extends diffusion pose prior modeling from body pose to expressive whole-body human pose.
    DPoser-X 先验：将 diffusion 姿态先验从身体扩展到包含手部和面部的表达性全身姿态。
  • Inverse-problem unification: treats pose-centric downstream tasks as inverse problems and solves them with variational diffusion sampling.
    逆问题统一建模：将多类姿态任务统一为逆问题，并通过 variational diffusion sampling 求解。
  • Truncated timestep scheduling: introduces a pose-specific diffusion sampling schedule to improve downstream task performance.
    截断时间步调度：提出适配姿态数据特性的采样时间步策略，提升下游任务效果。
  • Masked mixed-data training: combines whole-body and part-specific datasets under masking to capture cross-part dependencies while reducing action overfitting.
    掩码混合训练：通过掩码机制融合全身数据与局部数据，学习跨部位依赖并降低动作过拟合。

Method Overview

Diverse pose-centric tasks → inverse-problem formulation → diffusion prior sampling (DPoser-X) → whole-body pose solutions
Additional design: truncated timestep schedule + masked mixed-data training.

Why It Fits This Repo

  • Directly aligns with SMPL-X prior line: This is a whole-body pose prior method, conceptually adjacent to VPoser-style prior modeling but upgraded with diffusion.
    直接契合 SMPL-X 先验路线：这是全身姿态先验方法，可视为 VPoser 一类先验建模向 diffusion 路线的升级。
  • Reusable module value: The prior can be plugged into fitting/regression pipelines as a robust regularizer under ambiguous observations.
    可复用模块价值：该先验可作为拟合/回归管线中的鲁棒正则项，缓解观测模糊情况下的不稳定。

TL;DR

DPoser-X models whole-body human pose as a diffusion prior and solves pose tasks through inverse-problem sampling. Its key practical value in this paper bank is as a strong Optimization / Prior component for SMPL-X pipelines, especially when data coverage is incomplete across body, hand, and face.
